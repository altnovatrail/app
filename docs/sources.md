## 1. How to keep it

- **A record** is a subsection "N.M" of a topic: one source, or one reading of several sources for the sake of one decision. It holds the address, the version or the date of the page, the date read and the quote in the language of the source. What was not read in the primary source — a copy, a retelling, a page that did not open, an inference from code — the record names as such.
- **A reference.** A claim of a document refers to a record by its number, as to a section of any document: `(sources 5.1)`, and from another repository — `sources 5.1 app`. The number of a record does not change: a new record goes at the end of its topic, a new topic at the end of the file.
- **The source was read again** — an item with its own date is added to the record, and the earlier text is not edited: the decisions of its day rest on it. If the source changed and the claim diverged from it, that is an amendment to a decision or a question to the author, not an edit of the record.
- **What is not here:** the quotes that an entry of the decision log gives itself — they stay in the entry (RULES.md, How we work: a decision is never rewritten); the results of measurements — they are in [measurements.md](measurements.md).

### 3.1. Storage on the device, 2026-11-02

Read 2026-11-02 (architecture 3.2; R04). The quotes were checked with `curl` against the text of the pages:
- WHATWG, [Storage Standard](https://storage.spec.whatwg.org/), "Last Updated 19 October 2026": "The user agent cannot clear storage marked as persistent without involvement from the origin or user"; "A user agent that comes under storage pressure should clear network state and local storage buckets whose mode is "best-effort""; "Whenever a storage bucket is cleared by the user agent, it must be cleared in its entirety"; the quota "must not be a function of the available storage space on the device".
- MDN, [Storage quotas and eviction criteria](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Storage_API/Storage_quotas_and_eviction_criteria), "last modified on Jan 5, 2026":
  - Chromium — "an origin can store up to 60% of the total disk size in both persistent and best-effort modes"; Firefox without persistent storage — the smaller of "10% of the total disk size where the profile of the user is stored" and "10 GiB, which is the group limit"; Safari since macOS 14 and iOS 17 — "For WebKit-based browser apps, each origin can store up to around 60% of total disk";
  - "Browsers can store up to 5 MiB of local storage, and 5 MiB of session storage per origin"; beyond the quota a write "fails with a QuotaExceededError exception";
  - "In Firefox, when a site chooses to use persistent storage, the user is notified with a UI popup that their permission is requested.
