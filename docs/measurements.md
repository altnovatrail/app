## 1. Writing history with a mouse, 2026-11-10

How many bytes an attempt's writing history takes — for the estimate of how much space a work takes on the device.
- A measurement with a mouse, 2026-11-10 — the test of the built page in Chromium (`scripts/page.test.ts`): the answer "180 : 30 = 6, 6 + 1 = 7" in twenty strokes of 16 mouse moves 8 ms apart — 20 strokes, 360 points, 23,199 bytes of the history's JSON, about 64 bytes per point and 18 points per stroke.
- This is within the estimate of the storage on the device: work of hundreds of strokes is hundreds of KB, and the conclusion that no limit of its own is needed holds. With a pen and a finger the rate of points is higher — their measurement waits for devices in E3.

## 4. Three engines in CI, 2026-11-14

- **On what.** The builds of Playwright 1.65.0 in the container: Chrome Headless Shell 158.0.8127.18 (revision 1254), WebKit 27.2 (2388) and Firefox 159.0 (1572); `vitest` 5.1.0 with `@vitest/browser-playwright`. These are the engines on Linux, not Safari on an iPad or Firefox on Windows.

  - Firefox: `:lang()` matches nothing inside the shadow tree of the canvas element — neither by the host's `lang` nor by the page's: with the page and the host both `ru`, `:lang(ru)` and `:lang(en)` are false on the sheet's region.
