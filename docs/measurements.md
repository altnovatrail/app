# app — measurements

> The results of `app`'s measurements: what was measured, how, on what, and what numbers came out. The document that describes how things are built — [architecture.md](architecture.md) — links here and holds no numbers of measurements itself (RULES.md, How we work; R143 spec). A measurement is cited by its section, like any document: `measurements 2`, and from another repository `measurements 2 app`.
>
> An external fact that a measurement stands on is a record of [sources.md](sources.md). A new measurement is a new section at the end of the file, and a section's number does not change. Until T53, 2026-11-12, these results stood in architecture 3.2 and 4; the claims and the links to this file stayed there.

## 1. Writing history with a mouse, T25, 2026-11-10

How many bytes an attempt's writing history takes — for the estimate "How much space" of architecture 3.2.
- A measurement with a mouse, T25, 2026-11-10 — the test of the built page in Chromium (`scripts/page.test.ts`): the answer "180 : 30 = 6, 6 + 1 = 7" in twenty strokes of 16 mouse moves 8 ms apart — 20 strokes, 360 points, 23,199 bytes of the history's JSON, about 64 bytes per point and 18 points per stroke.
- This is within the estimate of architecture 3.2: work of hundreds of strokes is hundreds of KB, and the conclusion that no limit of its own is needed holds. With a pen and a finger the rate of points is higher — their measurement waits for devices in E3.

## 2. WebKit and Firefox, once, outside CI, T34, 2026-11-11

Checked 2026-11-11 — WebKit and Firefox, once, outside CI (T34; Д8, Д9, Д10 and Д13 in table 4 of RUN.md). The browsers — the builds of Playwright 1.64.1, WebKit 27.2 (`webkit-2370`) and Firefox 157 (`firefox-1555`), in a temporary folder, and their system libraries and fonts — from the Ubuntu snapshot `20261021T000000Z`, as for Chromium in the Dockerfile. These are the engines WebKit and Gecko on Linux, not Safari on an iPad:
- the tests in the browser, 331, and the test of the built page, 10, — with a copy of the Vitest configuration on WebKit and Firefox. In Firefox all passed, and one sign-in test only with a time per test above 15 s: Firefox is slower here. In WebKit three tests did not pass: writing a Blob into IndexedDB — twice —, and `forced-color-adjust`, which WebKit does not have. The checks tied to Chrome's error page in a frame, `chrome-error://chromewebdata/`, are each engine's own;
- **IndexedDB** (architecture 3.2): opening without creating, `versionchange`, the rejection on a new version, `blocked` on deletion, and a transaction whose work failed — as in Chromium, in both engines. WebKit does not write a Blob in an ephemeral session — a Playwright context without a profile runs as one: `put` fails with `UnknownError: Error preparing Blob/File data to be stored in object store`, while in a persistent profile of WebKit and in both kinds of context of Firefox the Blob is written. `app` then does not save the work, `unsaved`, and says so. `close` on erasing the site's data is covered by no test in either engine;
- **CSP** (architecture 3.7): the page starts under the lower layer and the header without violations, and Vite's modules and `modulepreload` work; an inline script and style inserted into a copy of the built `index.html` are blocked by the lower layer without the header in both engines, as in Chromium: WebKit — "Refused to execute a script because its hash, its nonce, or 'unsafe-inline' does not appear in the script-src directive", Firefox — "The page’s settings blocked an inline script (script-src-elem)"; the canvas element under the header sets its styles, shows an image by `blob:` and takes a stroke; under `frame-ancestors 'none'` the frame stays empty and does not ask for `/deployment.json`;
- **sign-in** (architecture 3.7): Firefox does not call Google until "Sign in with Google" is tapped. WebKit — with Safari named in `User-Agent` — at start loads the script `apis.google.com/js/api.js`, its module `gapi_iframes` from the same host and the hidden iframe `https://<authDomain>/__/auth/iframe`, as architecture 3.7 expects for Safari, — and without CSP violations: this is Google's real script, not a stub;
- **forced colors** (architecture 3.9): the project `forcedColors: "active"` passed in Firefox; WebKit has no such mode;
- **the language's name** (architecture 3.8): WebKit's `Intl.DisplayNames` gives «Английский» in Russian, with a capital letter.

## 3. The path in three engines, T57, 2026-11-14

The path of the first slice in a browser with no one at the keyboard — `just walk`, `walk/path.test.ts` (R44; architecture 3.7, "Tests"), — for Д13, Д14 and Д20 of DEBT.md.
- **On what.** The builds of Playwright 1.65.0 in the container — Chrome Headless Shell 158.0.8127.18 (revision 1254), WebKit 27.2 (2388) and Firefox 159.0 (1572) —, each in a new context, `en-US`, 1280 × 1200; the development server of `vite.config.ts` with `deployment.local.json` of the dev project of Firebase; `api` with its T74, whose container has the name `api`, by `API_DEV_APP_UID=dev-app-t57 just dev-app`: the stub of `tutor`, the plan `closed_slice`, the owner's profile «Dev» (R66 and R67 api). The sign-in is real Firebase Auth by the development token. No call of a real AI: the stub prices each call as the real one would, and spends the quota of the profile's day (R55 api).
- **Runs.** The whole path in the three engines, one after another: 20 tests of 21 in 235 s; the one that did not pass is WebKit's heartbeats below. Then WebKit alone twice, 7 of 7 each, about 70 s. The quota at each engine's start: Chromium 0%, WebKit 12%, Firefox 25%, then WebKit 38% and 51% — about 13% of $0.50, some $0.065, per engine and path, by the line «Tutor time today» itself.
- **What the engine gave.** Lessons of three modules — «Arithmetic with a trick: first steps» in Chromium, «Counting figures: first steps» in WebKit, «Cubes and paper: first steps» in Firefox —, each with three tasks among its last segments, ended by their solution; the first task of the skill map was in handwriting in every run. No task by tap came, so its answers were not walked.
- **What passed in all three engines.** My subjects with «Tutor time today: N% used»; a lesson — «Go on», back by its bar, a reload and «Continue the lesson»; the handwritten task as the stub reads its crop — a square crop «Can't read this», taller than wide «Not right yet: 1 item of 1» with the tutor's words and other words after «Explain differently», wider than tall «Correct»; «Show the solution», «Try it again» with an empty sheet, a reload with the 8 strokes kept, «There is a task in progress» and «Continue the task»; the section for adults — «Tutor time» with «This profile: N% used», the progress with the lesson's name and the term «Skills», «1 unfinished task» on the page of the device; «Sign out?» listing «Dev: 1 unfinished task», «Sign out and erase» with no database of the account left in `indexedDB.databases()`; «Erase everything on this device» with neither those nor `novatrail-settings` left.
- **The heartbeats of `@firebase/app`** (Д13). After «Erase everything on this device» the database `firebase-heartbeat-database` stays while the tab is open — the SDK's connection blocks its deletion, as R16 expects — and goes once the tab is closed: in Chromium and Firefox in each run; in WebKit, in the first run it was still there 15 s after the close, in the next two it was gone, the last 0.2 s after the close. A probe outside the path — sign-in, sign-out, a second sign-in, two reloads, the erasure, the close — saw it gone within 1 s in WebKit four times of four. Whether WebKit leaves it for good now and then, or only late, these runs do not tell; the walk waits 60 s.
- **What the path found.**
  - Two lines of a status ran together: "There is nothing more to offer for now" and the cause of the closed AI — «Today's tutor time is used up» — stood in one line with no full stop, on the screen of what's next and on the skill map, where «Choosing…» joined them too. Fixed in T57: the sentences are joined by a full stop.
  - In WebKit — with Safari named in `User-Agent`, where the SDK loads gapi and the hidden iframe at start (measurements 2) — the iframe's page on `<authDomain>` asks `https://www.googleapis.com/identitytoolkit/v3/relyingparty/getProjectConfig`, twice in a path. This address is not in the code of `@firebase/auth` 1.13.9: Google's page calls it. Chromium and Firefox did not ask it. Row В62 of RUN.md.
  - A lesson's first segment came about 1.3 s after it was opened, longer than the 1 s Vitest polls by default; the walk polls for 30 s.
- **Not checked here:** a task by tap; the words of a real model; a device — an iPad, Safari, Android.

## 4. Three engines in CI, T57, 2026-11-14

The tests of the screens and of the built page, and the site check, on Chromium, WebKit and Firefox (R45; architecture 3.1), for Д5, Д8–Д10 and Д13 of DEBT.md.
- **On what.** The builds of Playwright 1.65.0 in the container: Chrome Headless Shell 158.0.8127.18 (revision 1254), WebKit 27.2 (2388) and Firefox 159.0 (1572); `vitest` 5.1.0 with `@vitest/browser-playwright`. These are the engines on Linux, not Safari on an iPad or Firefox on Windows.
- **The run.** `just ci-test` in the container: Node and Chromium with the coverage — 85 files, 1,153 tests, 47 s, among them the test of the built page, 2 tests of the build and 13 per engine, and the site check's, 16 tests with the page in three engines; then the screens in WebKit and Firefox — 61 files, 1,119 tests and 1 skipped, 46 s. In CI the job "TypeScript / test" took 4 min 17 s with the three engines, against 1 min 56 s before them (pull requests 52 and 51).
- **What failed at first, of some 560 tests per engine, and what came of it:**
  - WebKit: reading a `Blob` of schema version 1 — the test cannot write one, since a context of Playwright is an ephemeral session (measurements 2). The test is not run in WebKit.
  - WebKit: the path "no voice" held that the engine lists no voice. WebKit lists four, flite's `kal` (`default`), `slt`, `rms` and `awb`, all `en-US` and all `localService: false`; it speaks an utterance — `start`, then `end`. `app` takes only voices the browser calls local, so its path is "no voice" there too, and the test now holds that no local voice is listed. Chromium and Firefox list none; Firefox ends an utterance with `error`.
  - Firefox: `:lang()` matches nothing inside the shadow tree of the canvas element — neither by the host's `lang` nor by the page's: with the page and the host both `ru`, `:lang(ru)` and `:lang(en)` are false on the sheet's region. Chromium and WebKit take the host's language. The test holds the region's name alone in Firefox (Д10).
  - Firefox: the box of each piece of the workbook's print, SVG text, is some 2 px wider than its scale gives, at the fit and at 200 % alike: the sum of the pieces grows 1.873 times at 200 %, the print from its leftmost piece to its rightmost 1.992 times — 1.996 in Chromium, 2.000 in WebKit. The test measures the latter.
  - The built page in WebKit, with Safari in `User-Agent`: at the start the SDK loads `/js/api.js` and its module `gapi_iframes` from `apis.google.com` and `/__/auth/iframe` with `iframe.js` from `authDomain` — Google's real files, with no violation of the CSP, as measurements 2 saw. The test records the page's own requests apart from Google's.
  - A frame under `frame-ancestors 'none'`: Chromium puts `chrome-error://chromewebdata/` in it, WebKit keeps app's address, `http://127.0.0.1:5181/`, with nothing run, Firefox leaves no address; none asks for `/deployment.json`.
  - The site check, on its test's stand-ins: under `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin` the window opened on `authDomain` in all three engines; once in WebKit the check stopped on the window's address, "Invalid URL", and in the next runs it did not: the check now names whatever address the window has.
- **The coverage** goes to Codecov from Node and Chromium alone: Vitest's v8 coverage refuses any other engine (sources 2.5).
